About Our System

The Multi-Tenant Boarding House Payment System simplifies monthly rent collection and record-keeping for boarding house owners and tenants. It centralizes payment records, reduces manual errors, and speeds up reconciliation so both owners and tenants have a clear, auditable history of payments.

Key features:

  • Tenant management and room assignment
  • Online payment records and receipts
  • Automated reports and notifications
  • Secure user roles for admins and tenants

Why use this system?

It saves time on manual bookkeeping, provides clear payment trails, and improves transparency between owners and tenants. The interface follows the same design language used across the app for consistency.
